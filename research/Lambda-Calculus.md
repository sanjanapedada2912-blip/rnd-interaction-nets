# 03 — Lambda Calculus 

## Why Are We Studying Lambda Calculus?

While exploring the **programming-language aspect** of our R&D topic, we started studying **Lambda Calculus**.

The purpose is to understand the theoretical foundations of computation, functions, substitution, and reduction.

Lambda Calculus is therefore being studied as a **related computational model**, rather than as the original focus of our research.

### 🔍 Topics Currently Being Explored


- Lambda expressions
- Variables
- Abstraction
- Function application
- Free variables
- Bound variables
- Substitution
- Alpha conversion
- Beta reduction
- Normal forms
- Evaluation
- Reduction strategies


### 🔗 Connection We Are Exploring

One of the questions arising from this research is:

> **How does computation expressed using Lambda Calculus relate to computation represented using Interaction Nets?**

We are currently investigating this relationship and have not yet reached a final conclusion.
